## How access works

- Every endpoint on every API is secured with OAuth 2.0, client_credentials grant.
- Token: POST https://prod01.api.gaig.com/oauth/token with Authorization: Basic base64(clientId:clientSecret),
  Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded, body grant_type=client_credentials. Tokens live 3599s.
- Call: Authorization: Bearer {access_token}
- Environments: dev01 / uat01 / prod01 .api.gaig.com, each with its own /oauth/token endpoint.
- There is NO self-service signup, no published pricing and no public sandbox. Credentials are issued
  through a four-phase engagement (Discovery, Business Specification, Development, Delivery/UAT), and the
  production token endpoint additionally rejects unrecognised source IPs at the network layer.
- Entitlement is per consumer. Call GET /api/endpoints first: it returns only the endpoints you may call.
  An unentitled endpoint answers 501 Not Implemented.
- Request and response bodies are NOT in the OpenAPI documents. POST /api/docs with
  {"type":"request|response","endpoint":"/api/…","format":"json","scope":{…}} returns the shape for your
  consumer, division, product and state.

## APIs

- [Shop](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/shop/index.html): workflow API orchestrating appetite, eligibility, application, pricing, documents and submit for new business.
- [Submission](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/submission/index.html): create, renew, read, update, assign, set status, search and bind new business submissions.
- [Rating](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/rating/index.html): rate or quote a product from locations, coverages, limits and deductibles; taxes.
- [Risk Selection](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/risk-selection/index.html): question-and-answer engine returning an appetite/eligibility outcome.
- [Product](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/product/index.html): class codes, program codes, symbols, categories, availability, class of business.
- [Issuance](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/issuance/index.html): straight-through issue, enroll, re-enroll, cancel, policy mod; mints policy and customer numbers.
- [Policy](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/policy/index.html): read policy, quote, enrollment, certificate, cancel dates and transactions.
- [Forms](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/forms/index.html): search, attach and print policy forms; JSON or XML; ACORD form attachment intake datatype.
- [Document](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/document/index.html): ECM document storage, versions, annotations, comments and underwriting cases.
- [Billing](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/billing/index.html): payments, billing accounts, payment plans, bill charges, producer updates, One Inc acknowledgements.
- [Ingestion](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/ingestion/index.html): send Great American JSON, XML, PDF or binary; multipart ingest; configured inbound webhook receivers.
- [Risk Assessment](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/risk-assessment/index.html): address validation and geocode, BCEGS, protection class, cat modeling, wildfire/flood/hail/wind/coastal/sinkhole scores, crime score, motor carrier and driver reports, sanction compliance, property valuation, VIN lookup.
- [Claims](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/claims/index.html): first notice of loss create/update/submit, risk reports, payment reporting, service providers.
- [Notification](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/notification/index.html): claims notification opt in/out, reopen, close, messages, notes; chat start and transcript.
- [Producer](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/producer/index.html): producer, agency code, business unit, hierarchy, contacts, claims and billing info.
- [Contract](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/contract/index.html): submit, retrieve and search contract and tracking data.
- [Opportunity](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/opportunity/index.html): create, read, update, delete and search opportunity records.
- [Letters](https://api-documentation.gaig.com/letters/index.html): printable quote letters, quote letter data and reports.
